# Facts to Verify Before Publishing

## Corporate
Legal name, founding history, current line count, current capacity, official address/channels.

## Products
Names, grades, diameter ranges, PN, SDR, standards, restrictions, markings, packaging, MOQ, lead time.

## Gas
Applicable standards, approvals/vendor status and permitted claims.

## Laboratory
Accreditation status, certificate number, issuing body, validity, scope, tests, external-service availability.

## Projects
Client/project/location/year/product/DN/PN/grade/length/publication permission/photos.

## Export
Actual markets, Iraq capability, terms, MOQ, packaging/loading, documentation, lead time.

## Dealers
Official list and verification data.

## SEO/domain
Control of bukanpipe.com, role of bukanpipe.ir, Search Console, analytics, indexed URLs, backlinks.
