# Export Strategy

Sequence:
1. Persian
2. English corporate/technical/export
3. Iraq-specific export landing
4. Arabic
5. Sorani only after demand validation

Iraq pages should be commercial, not simple translations: products, standards, range, packing, loading, MOQ, delivery capability, documentation, contacts and datasheets.

Preconditions: actual export capability, legal/commercial feasibility, logistics, target buyers, pricing and documentation.
