# Content Strategy

Move from a generic blog to a **Bukan Pipe Knowledge Center**.

## Pillars
- Product selection
- Water
- Agriculture
- Gas
- Laboratory
- Installation/welding

## Acceptance rule
Every asset must do at least one of:
1. Search
2. Sales
3. Trust

If it does none, do not publish it.

## Legacy content policy
Classify: KEEP / UPDATE / MERGE / REDIRECT / REMOVE with documented reason.

## Case studies
Where permission exists, include sector, location, problem, product, DN, PN, PE grade, length, year, QA/testing context, outcome and real imagery.
