# English SERP Research — Phase 008

Research date: 2026-09-03  
Purpose: Inform native English keyword targeting and page-type decisions (not to copy competitor claims).

## Method

Representative queries were reviewed for:

- Page type ranking (manufacturer home, product category, educational guide, distributor)
- Terminology in titles and H1s
- Common buyer questions in PAA / related searches
- What **not** to infer as Bukan Pipe facts

## Query findings

### HDPE pipe manufacturer / PE pipe manufacturer

| Observation | Implication for Bukan Pipe EN |
|-------------|-------------------------------|
| SERP mixes manufacturer homepages, regional suppliers, and marketplace listings | Home + About own **manufacturer** intent; avoid unverified “global exporter” language |
| Titles use “HDPE pipe manufacturer” + location or product scope | EN home hero: manufacturer + water/gas/irrigation scope |
| Buyers expect factory credibility, standards, contact | About, Quality, Certifications, Contact support commercial pages |

### HDPE water pipe / PE100 water pipe

| Observation | Implication |
|-------------|-------------|
| Product/category pages rank alongside spec tables | `/en/products/water-supply-pipe` owns **HDPE water pipe** commercial intent |
| “PE100 water pipe” often appears in body/supporting copy, not always H1 | Secondary keyword on water product page |
| Pressure, SDR, PN mentioned as selection factors | Technical Center + pillar own educational intent |

### HDPE gas pipe / PE100 gas pipe

| Observation | Implication |
|-------------|-------------|
| Strong association with distribution, electrofusion, EN 1555 | Gas product page primary: **HDPE gas pipe**; welding article supports jointing |
| Compliance language is standard-specific | Only claim EN 1555 / INSO 11223 / IGS where verified for Bukan production |

### HDPE irrigation pipe

| Observation | Implication |
|-------------|-------------|
| Agricultural mains vs emitter systems split across results | Irrigation **product** vs subsurface **application/article** separation |
| “Agricultural HDPE pipe” common in farm supply context | Application page targets use-case; product page targets procurement |

### PE100 pipe

| Observation | Implication |
|-------------|-------------|
| Material-grade intent distinct from application pages | Dedicated `/en/products/pe100-pipe` |
| Educational content covers MRS, SDR, derating | Pillar + temperature article — avoid cannibalizing product page |

### Polyethylene pipe testing / ISO 17025 laboratory

| Observation | Implication |
|-------------|-------------|
| Laboratory service pages rank for testing intent | Standalone `/en/laboratory/*` section |
| Accreditation claims are certificate-specific | Do **not** state current ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation without valid certificate |

## Terminology decisions

| Use on EN site | Avoid as default |
|----------------|------------------|
| HDPE pipe, polyethylene pipe | “PE pipe systems” stuffed in every H1 |
| PE100 pipe, PE100 water/gas pipe | PE80/PE100 interchange without project context |
| Butt fusion, electrofusion | Literal calques from Persian |
| PN, SDR, MOP | Unverified ASTM/ISO compliance claims for Bukan |
| Request a Quote, Contact sales | “Global leader”, “world-class”, “30+ years” without date math |

## Page-type mapping (SERP-aligned)

| Intent | Owner URL |
|--------|-----------|
| Manufacturer / brand | `/en`, `/en/about` |
| Product commercial | `/en/products/*` |
| Use-case engineering | `/en/applications/*` |
| Educational / selection | `/en/polyethylene-pipe`, `/en/technical-center/*` |
| Testing services | `/en/laboratory/*` |
| Quote conversion | `/en/request-quote`, spec CTAs on products |

## Cannibalization guardrails

- **Pillar** targets educational “HDPE pipe / polyethylene pipe / PE100” — not “HDPE pipe manufacturer”
- **Products hub** supports catalog navigation — primary keywords stay on individual product URLs
- **Applications** must not duplicate product body copy or primary keywords

## Sources reviewed (public SERPs, not copied)

Industrial manufacturer sites, standards bodies’ editorial pages, and distributor category pages for terminology patterns only. No competitor specifications or certifications were imported as Bukan facts.
