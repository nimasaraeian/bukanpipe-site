# Project Charter

## Purpose
Create a new, SEO-led, high-performance industrial digital platform for Bukan Pipe.

This is not a visual refresh. It is a controlled rebuild designed to create durable digital authority and measurable commercial demand.

## Business outcomes
1. Increase qualified inbound RFQs.
2. Improve organic visibility for high-intent polyethylene-pipe searches.
3. Turn the laboratory into both a trust asset and inbound acquisition channel.
4. Make product selection easier for engineers, contractors, buyers and agricultural customers.
5. Establish Bukan Pipe as a credible technical authority.
6. Protect the brand against impersonation / unauthorized representation.
7. Prepare future English / Iraq / Arabic expansion.

## Governance
- Strategy/architecture/SEO/review: senior project lead.
- Implementation: Cursor, one approved phase at a time.
- Business truth: factory management and current documents.

## Quality rule
> No industrial claim without evidence.
