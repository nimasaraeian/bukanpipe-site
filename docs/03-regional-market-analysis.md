# Regional Market Analysis

## Regional opportunity
Bukan Pipe's north-west location should be translated into measurable advantages rather than vague geography marketing:
- shorter delivery routes in regional projects
- faster response
- agricultural demand
- regional project familiarity
- access to western trade corridors

## Agriculture
Initial public research indicates a meaningful agricultural base in Bukan and substantial adoption of modern irrigation in West Azerbaijan. Agriculture should therefore be treated as a full vertical, not a small product card.

Recommended hub: `/applications/agriculture/`

Supporting topics: irrigation mains, pressure irrigation, drip systems, flow, pressure loss, diameter selection, pump support, installation, maintenance and real projects.

## Iraq / Kurdistan
Proximity to western trade corridors makes Iraq/Kurdistan a plausible later-stage export opportunity, but it must be validated commercially before investment.

Recommended sequence:
1. Persian excellence
2. English corporate/export layer
3. Iraq-specific landing
4. Arabic
5. Sorani only if demand justifies it
