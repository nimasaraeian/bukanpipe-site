## Brief for the photographer

1. Capture **raw or high-quality JPEG**, long edge ≥ 4000px where practical.
2. Keep pipes geometrically true — no ultra-wide distortion of diameters.
3. Light the bore and cut ends; avoid mixed fluorescent colour casts where possible.
4. Provide both **wide environmental** and **tight detail** of the same subject for art direction.
5. People only with consent and without implying unearned accreditation.
6. Do not watermark.
7. Deliver color-managed files named by subject, e.g. `bukan-pipe-extrusion-line-wide.jpg`.
