# 90-Day Roadmap

## Days 1–3 — Discovery
Canonical domain, legacy URL/content inventory, evidence request, Search Console/analytics access, migration risks.

## Days 1–5 — Greenfield foundation
Next.js/React/TypeScript, RTL base, lint/build, SEO utilities, content types, architecture docs.

## Days 4–7 — SEO/IA
Final routes, page intent, keyword mapping, redirect skeleton, internal-link graph.

## Week 2 — Design system
Typography, grid, spacing, UI tokens, responsive/accessibility rules.

## Weeks 2–4 — Core commercial site
Home, products, product detail, RFQ, lab, projects, about, contact.

## Weeks 3–4 — Technical SEO
Metadata, canonicals, robots, sitemap, schema, 404, redirects, semantics, images, CWV.

## Weeks 4–5 — Trust/lab
Laboratory hub, accreditation, verified tests, standards, certificates, projects, dealer strategy.

## Weeks 5–6 — Lead generation
Structured RFQ, attribution, conversion events, BOQ upload, sales handoff.

## Weeks 6–10 — Content authority
Legacy refresh + commercial/engineering/agriculture/water/gas/lab clusters.

## Weeks 8–11 — Engineering tools
Product Finder, weight, SDR/PN, flow, pressure-loss candidates.

## Weeks 11–12 — Launch
SEO/mobile/accessibility/performance/redirect/analytics QA, cutover, monitoring.
