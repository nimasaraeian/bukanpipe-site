# Competitive Landscape

## Three competitor classes
1. Manufacturers
2. Commercial/distributor sites
3. Technical/content sites

## Initial benchmarks from research
- **Parsian Pipe:** standards, product authority, multilingual/export, Iraq content.
- **Yazd Polymer:** long-tail SEO and diameter-specific pages.
- **Avand Plast:** documentation, quality and quotation flows.
- **Kabir Looleh:** clear specifications and CTA.
- **Mahco:** brand/engineering benchmark, R&D/lab/English presence.
- **Commercial price players:** capture transactional SERPs with detailed price/specification tables.

## Competitive lesson
Do not try to win by claiming to be the biggest. Win through proof, technical confidence, conversion quality and category depth.

## Wedge
**Factory + Laboratory + Engineering + Projects + RFQ**
