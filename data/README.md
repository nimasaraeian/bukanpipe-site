# Local content data

This folder holds structured, typed records until a CMS is approved.

Rules:
- Every published record must be verified.
- Empty collections are valid.
- Do not copy unverified claims from the legacy website.

`data/migration/` is the legacy URL inventory for future redirects. It is not publishable product content. Redirects stay disabled (`ENABLE_LEGACY_REDIRECTS=false`).

`data/media/` is the legacy photography/document inventory and grade board. It is not a product catalog. Do not treat a C-grade still as a campaign hero.
